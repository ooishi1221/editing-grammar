## Possible near-duplicate names

- `summary` and `recap`: the former supports structured reference or process compression; the latter denotes a retention-oriented revisit.
- `progress-ui` and `current-state`: the former identifies a live-progress interface; the latter identifies the cognitive task of locating the present stage.
- `continuity` and `transition`: continuity is the desired perceptual result, while transition identifies the handoff treatment.
- `afterglow` and `pause`: afterglow is the intended emotional residue; pause is a temporal treatment.

No merge is made in this phase. These pairs need evidence from a later Search v2 or recommendation review.

## Difficult classifications

- **VS-B04:** its source title is appearance-led, so `tone` is retained as the stable decision concept rather than adding a source-style tag.
- **VS-L05:** `b-roll` remains singleton because it distinguishes supplemental subject footage from layout or reaction treatments.
- **VS-I05:** `numeric-emphasis` remains singleton because it separates memorability of one value from comparison across values.
- **VS-T05:** `inner-voice` remains singleton because it distinguishes unspoken thought from annotation or ordinary captioning.
