# Product Identity Pattern v0.2

## Editorial job

VS-I15 商品同定・パックショット makes an exact supplied product or package
the identifiable primary item of information. It is appropriate when accurate
product identity is the editorial job, rather than merely a supporting visual.

## Evidence

The reusable abstraction follows repeated visually verified commercial
observations:

- [C01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m_lThKHsOKA) /
  O-C01-028 at 00:28: a food-product pack shot.
- [C03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SgdAE_6iK80) /
  O-C03-028 at 00:28: a product/full-product pack shot.
- [C04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K7DT1uY3QXo) /

Google/YouTube [G03](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551?hl=en),
ABCDs of effective video ads, supports product visibility, brand identification,
and reducing competing elements. It is advertising guidance and does not
prescribe universal geometry. Thinkbox
[G10](https://www.thinkbox.tv/how-to-use-tv/sponsorship-and-content/tv-sponsorship/tv-sponsorship-creative-guidelines),
TV sponsorship creative guidelines, supports clear product, pack-shot, and
logo identification; its scope is sponsorship creative and likewise does not
generalize exact execution to all commercials.

## Distinctness

- **VS-L05 B-roll差し込み** shows related supporting material and preserves
  narration continuity. It does not require an exact product identity to become
  the informational subject.
- **VS-B01 チャンネルマーク** identifies a publisher, channel, or brand source;
  it does not identify a particular product or package.
- **VS-C06 エンド画面** presents destinations and actions at an end state; it
  does not establish product identity.

## Pattern vs CF-06

VS-I15 expresses why exact product identification is required. CF-06
product-identity expresses a reusable composition relationship in which that
product is primary. They are independent selections: neither automatically
selects or requires the other.

## Provenance

The cited frames remain observed research data. VS-I15 is an
inferred/proposed library abstraction: its title, category, applicability, and
implementation grammar are not asserted as source facts.
